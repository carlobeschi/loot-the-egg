Loot the Egg is a 3D game that runs in your browser. Pick a class, climb spooky dungeon towers, beat the boss and grab its egg. But the moment you do, everything wakes up and chases you.

Play nowHow it works

No download. Best on a computer with a keyboard and mouse. Your progress saves in your browser.

How it works

One loop, and it never gets old.

1

Pick a class

Knight, Wizard or Bowman. Each has its own weapon and its own special move.

2

Beat a boss

Climb a dungeon tower floor by floor. The boss waits at the very top.

3

Grab the egg

The boss drops an egg. Take it and every monster wakes up and hunts you. Run back to town!

4

Hatch pets

Eggs hatch into pets that live in your pen and earn coins for you, even while you fight.

Three classes

Every class gets 12 weapons to buy and upgrade.

🛡️

Knight

Close-range fighter with the biggest health.

  • Weapon: sword that sweeps in an arc
  • Special: Shield Slam, a shockwave that shoves enemies back
🔮

Wizard

Magic bolts that burst and hit whole groups.

  • Weapon: staff with glowing crystals
  • Special: Blink Burst, teleport forward and leave an explosion behind
🏹

Bowman

Fast and fragile. Arrows pierce several enemies.

  • Weapon: bow that gets longer and brighter with each tier
  • Special: Arrow Volley, a fan of piercing arrows

Dungeon towers and two worlds

Beat a dungeon to unlock the next one on the map. Towers are scattered far apart, so you have to explore.

14dungeon towers across both worlds
2–5floors per tower, joined by stairs
18dungeon depths to clear
12weapon tiers per class

World 1: the Green Forest

Five dungeon towers to start, with a cozy town, a shop, a blacksmith and your pet pen. Enemies get tougher with every depth.

World 2: the Ember Wastes

Nine more towers with five floors each, lava pools, tougher bosses and rarer eggs. Unlock the World Gate in town to get there.

Bosses fight back

Ground slams with a red warning ring, helpers called to the fight, and an enrage when they’re hurt.

New zombies

Runners are fast, tanks are armored, and every mob has glowing eyes and claws.

Spooky inside

The sky goes dark as you enter a tower: torches, chandeliers, pointed windows and bones.

Eight egg rarities

Deeper dungeons drop rarer eggs, and Common eggs get very unlikely down there. You can only get eggs from bosses, never from a shop. The number shows the coins one pet earns every second.

Pets that look the part

16 different pets, from a tiny Chick to the Angel Dragon and the Star Phoenix.

A pen that levels up

Your pen changes look with every upgrade, from wood and stone to gold, gems and a rainbow fence.

Rebirth

Reborn 12 times to multiply your coins up to x7, and you keep all of your pets.

Questions

Does it save my progress?

Yes, in your own browser. It won’t follow you to another device or another browser.

Can I play on my phone?

Not well yet. It was made for a keyboard and mouse.

Is it multiplayer?

No, it’s a single-player game for now.

What if the game doesn’t start?

It needs WebGL and an internet connection to load. Try a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, and reload the page.
